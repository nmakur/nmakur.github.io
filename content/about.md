## Books

I've been reading books a bit too much ever since I was little. My interests tend to lean towards fantasy books. The books that I've easily reread at least 10 times are:
- <color val="yellow">Percy Jackson and the Olympians</color> and generally most of Rick Riordan's work, though I haven't kept up with the later books
- <color val="yellow">Harry Potter</color>
- <color val="yellow">Artemis Fowl</color>

Some less-read (but still great!) books from my childhood:
- <color val="yellow">The Five Find-Outers</color> by Enid Blyton
- <color val="yellow">The Boxcar Children</color> by Gertrude Warner

More sciency books that I enjoyed when I was first exploring:

Finally, books that I really should get around to reading:
