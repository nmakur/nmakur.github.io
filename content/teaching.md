### 21-237 F26
- [Recitation 1](/teaching/21237_f26_reci_1.pdf): An introduction to fundamental groups and to free groups, with an eye towards category-theoretic thinking.
- [Recitation 2](/teaching/21237_f26_reci_2.pdf): Applications of group actions to finding normal subgroups and proving a map is surjective if and only if it is an epimorphism.
- [Recitation 3](/teaching/21237_f26_reci_3.pdf): Quotient groups and their application to prove the 5/8ths theorem. Some relevant exercises to the recitation topics can be found [here](/teaching/exer_3_21237_f26.pdf).
- [Recitation 4](/teaching/21237_f26_reci4.pdf): The universal property of quotients, the abelianization, and automorphisms of the symmetric group.
